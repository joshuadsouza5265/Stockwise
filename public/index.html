ZERO JARGON, MORE CLARITY

Learn stocks.

A quick-start guide for new investors and market nerds.

01 / BASICS

What is a stock?

A stock represents a small ownership stake in a company. Its market price changes as buyers and sellers react to expectations, results, news and wider conditions.

02 / PRICE ACTION

Why prices move

Prices can react to earnings, guidance, company announcements, rates, currency moves, sector trends and investor positioning. A headline near a price move is not automatically its cause.

03 / RISK

Risk before reward

Stocks can lose value, including all the money invested in a company that fails. Diversification may reduce concentration risk but cannot eliminate market risk.

04 / TECHNICALS

RSI, MACD & averages

RSI summarizes recent momentum, MACD compares moving averages, and SMAs smooth past prices. They describe history; none can reliably predict the next move.

05 / FUNDAMENTALS

P/E and earnings

The P/E ratio compares share price with earnings per share. Compare companies within similar industries and check debt, cash flow, growth and accounting context too.

06 / PRACTICE

Paper trade first

Use the simulator to test a plan, record why you entered and review results over time. Simulated fills and emotions differ from real markets.

Educational content only, not personalized financial advice. Consider your goals, time horizon and risk tolerance; verify data before making decisions.
